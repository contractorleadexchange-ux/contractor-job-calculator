Why productive hours matter

An employee can be paid for 2,080 hours in a year without producing 2,080 billable hours. Paid holidays, vacation, sick time, meetings, training, shop time, travel and other nonbillable activity reduce the number of hours available to recover the employee’s cost.

That is why this calculator shows both loaded cost per paid hour and true cost per productive hour.
