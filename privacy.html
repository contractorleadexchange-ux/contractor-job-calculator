How this site handles information.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Calculator inputs

The job calculator is designed to perform its calculations in your web browser. The amounts you type into the calculator are not intentionally submitted to Contractor Job Calculator as part of the calculation itself.

Website and hosting data

Like most websites, hosting, security, analytics or advertising providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, timestamps and requested pages. This information may be used for security, performance measurement, fraud prevention, analytics and website operation.

Advertising and cookies

This site may use Google AdSense or other advertising services. If advertising is enabled, advertising providers may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to deliver, measure and personalize ads where permitted. Google and its partners may process information about visits to this and other websites for advertising purposes.

Where required, the site will use an appropriate consent mechanism for advertising cookies and personalized advertising. You can also manage browser cookie settings and Google advertising preferences through Google's own controls.

Analytics

The site may use analytics tools to understand aggregate usage such as page views, device type and calculator engagement. Analytics configuration may change as the site develops.

Third-party links

The site links to the Washington Department of Revenue, Contractor Lead Exchange and other third-party resources. Those websites have their own privacy practices and policies.
