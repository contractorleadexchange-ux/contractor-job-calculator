    How the calculator works

    Source: CLX-provided 2026 planning ranges for Southwest Washington, in the context of typical roofs of approximately 1,800–2,500 sq ft. These are CLX inputs, not independently verified market averages.

    Roofing typeOriginal CLX range
    Basic 3-tab asphalt$9,000–$13,000
    Architectural asphalt$14,000–$20,000
    High-end architectural$18,000–$28,000
    Standing-seam metal$25,000–$45,000+

    Size assumption: The calculator anchors those ranges to a 2,000 sq ft reference roof, a modeling choice within the typical size band. The supplied figures do not establish a measured cost-per-square-foot rate. Other sizes scale proportionally: original range × (roof area ÷ 2,000). The low end rounds down and the high end rounds up to the nearest $500. Fixed costs and job conditions do not scale evenly.

    Scope: This is for roof replacement planning, not repairs, a material-only price, or a remote roof measurement. Condition selections generate inspection questions and leave the numeric range unchanged. Compare written contractor estimates for a confirmed scope and price.
