How this calculator handles Washington contractor tax

Prime/custom construction: the calculator treats the contract as retail construction, estimates retail sales tax on the contract amount, and uses the Washington retailing B&O rate of 0.471%.

Subcontractor with a valid reseller permit: the calculator estimates no retail sales tax collected from the prime contractor and uses the wholesaling B&O rate of 0.484%.

Subcontractor without a valid reseller permit: the calculator treats the transaction as retail for this simplified estimate, applies the entered sales-tax rate, and uses retailing B&O.

Actual tax treatment can vary with the project, customer, exemptions, deductions and construction classification. Verify tax treatment with the Washington Department of Revenue construction tax matrix or a qualified tax professional.
